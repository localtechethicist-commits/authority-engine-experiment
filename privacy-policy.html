Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 June 2026

This policy explains how We Build Proper (a subsidiary of Euforia Singapore Pte Ltd) collects, uses, and protects the personal data you provide through the Authority Engine™ diagnostic tool. We comply with Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA) and the Spam Control Act.

1. Who We Are

We Build Proper is a brand strategy and positioning consultancy, operating as a subsidiary of Euforia Singapore Pte Ltd.

For data protection enquiries, contact us at: marketing@webuildproper.com.sg

2. What Data We Collect

When you use the Authority Engine™ diagnostic, we collect:

  • Name — to personalise your results and communications
  • Email address — to send you your diagnostic report and follow-up communications (only with your explicit consent)
  • Quiz answers — 19 questions about your business, used to calculate your Business Owner Archetype and Authority Score
  • Archetype result and score — the output of your diagnostic, stored to personalise follow-up
  • Usage data — standard analytics (page visits, quiz completion, traffic source via Google Analytics 4) to improve the tool

We do not collect payment information, NRIC, or sensitive personal data.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to:

  • Deliver your Authority Engine™ diagnostic result
  • Send you marketing communications about business strategy, growth insights, and our services — only if you have given explicit consent
  • Segment leads by archetype to personalise follow-up communications
  • Improve our diagnostic tool and marketing effectiveness (aggregate, anonymised data only)
  • Contact you if you requested a strategy session

We do not sell your data to third parties.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data on the basis of:

  • Consent — you explicitly checked the consent box before submitting your details
  • Legitimate interest — to deliver the diagnostic service you requested

5. Who We Share Data With

Your data may be shared with the following service providers who assist us in operating the diagnostic:

  • Google (Analytics 4 + Sheets) — analytics and lead storage
  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram) — advertising attribution via Conversions API (hashed data only)
  • LinkedIn — advertising attribution via Insight Tag
  • Railway — backend hosting for data processing
  • Vercel — website hosting

All third-party providers are bound by their own privacy policies and data processing agreements.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for:

  • Lead data (name, email, quiz results) — up to 24 months from your last interaction, or until you request deletion
  • Analytics data — 14 months (Google Analytics default)

After this period, data is deleted or anonymised.

7. Your Rights Under PDPA

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate data
  • Withdrawal of consent — withdraw your consent to marketing communications at any time
  • Deletion — request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal obligations)
  • Data portability — request your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, email us at marketing@webuildproper.com.sg. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Unsubscribing from Marketing Emails

Every marketing email we send includes an unsubscribe link. You may also email us directly at marketing@webuildproper.com.sg to opt out. We will process opt-out requests within 10 business days (we aim for immediate processing).

9. Cookies and Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use our site. This may set cookies on your device. GA4 anonymises IP addresses and does not identify individuals. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

We also use Meta Pixel and LinkedIn Insight Tag for advertising measurement. These may set third-party cookies. You can manage ad preferences via Meta Ad Settings and LinkedIn Ad Settings.

10. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data, including HTTPS encryption, hashed transmission of data to advertising platforms, and restricted access to lead data.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the Authority Engine™ after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact Us

For any questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your data:

  • Email: marketing@webuildproper.com.sg
  • Company: We Build Proper (subsidiary of Euforia Singapore Pte Ltd)
  • Jurisdiction: Singapore

If you believe we have not handled your data correctly, you may lodge a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC).
