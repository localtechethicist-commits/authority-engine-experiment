The Builder Behind The Framework

Developed by Business Builders Who Have Worked Across Branding, Growth, and Business Transformation

We Build Proper exists to solve a problem most agencies avoid naming:

Most businesses do not have a marketing problem.
They have a positioning problem.

The Authority Engine™️ was developed by Melvin Ho through direct work with SMEs, founders, and growth-stage companies across branding, marketing, and business transformation engagements.

It was not created as a theoretical model. It was built from repeated real-world patterns observed across industries — where businesses were investing in marketing activity, but still struggling to achieve consistent growth.

The root cause was rarely execution. It was clarity.

Businesses without clear positioning consistently fall into the same pattern:

  • They compete on price instead of value.
  • They rely on referrals instead of demand generation.
  • They scale activity without improving conversion quality.

This pattern became the foundation of the Authority Engine™️ — a structured diagnostic system designed to identify the true constraint behind business growth and rebuild the clarity, positioning, and authority required for scale.

About Melvin Ho

Melvin Ho is a business builder and brand strategist focused on helping companies solve positioning and growth constraints at a structural level, rather than through tactical marketing execution.

His work spans branding, marketing strategy, and business transformation projects with SMEs and growth-stage companies across multiple industries in Singapore and the region.

Rather than focusing on surface-level marketing outputs, his approach centres on diagnosing the underlying business clarity issues that affect demand, conversion, and long-term market perception.

This perspective forms the basis of the Authority Engine™️ — a framework built to help business owners understand what is actually limiting their growth, and how to rebuild a stronger market position from the ground up.

Today, We Build Proper works with founders and leadership teams who are not looking for more marketing activity, but for strategic clarity that changes how their business competes in the market.
